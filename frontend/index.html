Why Reviewr Exists

The Feedback Fragmentation Problem & Our Solution

How Reviewr transforms chaotic, multi-channel customer noise into quantified operational growth.

The Legacy Problem

Fragmented Customer Feedback Chaos

✕
Siloed Data Channels: Reviews scattered across Google Maps locations, YouTube videos, Play Store versions, and Instagram reels without a central data lake.
✕
Unstructured Text Overload: Thousands of free-form customer comments with slang, emojis, mixed languages, and nuanced sarcasm that traditional keyword alerts miss.
✕
Zero Branch Attribution: Executives cannot isolate whether dining delays belong to the T. Nagar branch or Velachery branch without manual reading.
✕
Stale Sentiment Metrics: Static average star ratings mask emerging operational crises (e.g. food quality dips or software update crashes).
The Reviewr Solution

Unified VoC & NLP Intelligence Engine

✓
Multi-Source Automated Ingestion: Official REST API connectors ingest Google Places reviews, YouTube comment threads/replies, and Play Store reviews into a unified schema.
✓
Aspect-Based Sentiment (ABSA): Sub-sentence polarity scoring decomposes comments into specific attributes (e.g. "Food was great (+0.92)" vs "Waiting queue was slow (-0.78)").
✓
Reviewr AI Theme Clustering: Semantic embeddings cluster millions of reviews into real-time operational themes with quantified trajectory curves.
✓
Automated Recommendation Engine: Discovers delight drivers, alerts branch managers of friction points, and recommends revenue-expanding fixes.
End-to-End Processing Architecture

How Reviewr Ingests & Processes Unstructured Feedback

From raw API ingestion to aspect-level sentiment classification and executive action triggers.

1

Multi-Source API Fetch

Asynchronous HTTPX client fetches public reviews and nested comment replies via official REST APIs with quota tracking.

HTTPX / AsyncIO
2

Normalization & Dedup

Standardizes dates, author avatars, star ratings, and enforces composite uniqueness on (business_id, source, external_id).

PostgreSQL JSONB
3

NLP Cleaning & Tokens

Strips HTML markup, handles emoji polarity mapping, detects language, and performs lemmatization on review text.

NLTK / RegEx
4

Aspect-Based Sentiment

VADER and fine-tuned Transformer models calculate continuous polarity (-1.0 to +1.0) and map to Positive, Neutral, or Negative tiers.

VADER / ABSA
5

Themes & Actions

Semantic topic clustering groups feedback into high-impact business themes and synthesizes automated operational recommendations.

BERTopic & Rules
